# NPC Tables

Each entry names a kind of detail and leaves the specifics open. Fill them in to fit the setting, the NPC's role, and the campaign, so the same roll never produces the same NPC twice.

Roll on these with `scripts/roll_npc.py`, or pick from them by hand.

## Appearance

| d12 | Prompt                                                             |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | A scar - where it is, and what left it                             |
| 2   | Something missing - a finger, an eye, teeth - and what replaced it |
| 3   | Clothing that doesn't match their station, too fine or too worn    |
| 4   | A mark left by their trade - stains, burns, calluses, dust         |
| 5   | Jewellery or a keepsake they never take off, and who it came from  |
| 6   | A sign of poor health they are hiding                              |
| 7   | Hair or grooming that took real effort, in one specific way        |
| 8   | A weapon or tool carried where everyone can see it                 |
| 9   | Something about their build that surprises people                  |
| 10  | A recent injury, and the story they tell about it                  |
| 11  | A symbol of a faith, guild, or house, worn openly or hidden        |
| 12  | Something that doesn't belong to this place at all                 |
