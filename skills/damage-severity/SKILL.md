# Damage Severity

Pick a severity based on what the effect should do to characters of the party's level, then use the average or one of the dice options from the table.

- **Minor:** a scrape or a burn that stings. It never threatens a healthy character.
- **Setback:** unlikely to kill or seriously harm a healthy character.
- **Dangerous:** likely to seriously injure, and could kill, a character who is already hurt.
- **Deadly:** likely to kill a character who is already hurt, and can drop a healthy one.

## Damage

## Save DCs and Attack Bonuses

For a saving throw or attack roll against the effect, use the matching DC or attack bonus:

| Severity  | Save DC | Attack Bonus |
| --------- | ------- | ------------ |
| Minor     | 10      | +3           |
| Setback   | 10-12   | +3 to +5     |
| Dangerous | 13-15   | +6 to +8     |
| Deadly    | 16-18   | +9 to +11    |

## Examples

Use these to pick a severity for an effect. The damage then comes from the table for the party's level.

| Severity  | Examples                                                                                  |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Minor     | Grabbing a hot pan, a shelf of jars toppling onto you, a splinter-trap in a latch         |
| Setback   | A stove bursting open, falling through a rotten floor into a cellar, a swinging log       |
| Dangerous | A mine ceiling caving in, being dragged through a burning building, a scything blade      |
| Deadly    | A portcullis crashing down on you, wading into molten metal, caught under a falling tower |
